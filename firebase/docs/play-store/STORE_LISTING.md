## Full description

GroupTown helps people explore homes, compare ownership options, and participate in organised housing groups.

With GroupTown you can:

- browse available homes and property interests;
- compare indicative prices, payment terms, monthly contributions, and group-size options;
- save your interest and complete registration at your own pace;
- join an eligible open group or request to create a new group;
- follow group progress, milestones, documents, and finance records;
- contribute securely through the Hubtel checkout when payments are enabled;
- view completed, pending, failed, and cancelled payment activity;
- share updates, photos, videos, and documents with the Community or your group;
- chat with group members and respond to posts;
- receive account, group, support, announcement, and payment notifications; and
- contact support when you need help.

GroupTown is intended for adults aged 18 and above. Property availability, pricing, payment schedules, approvals, and delivery information may change. Final commitments are governed by the applicable programme and transaction documents. GroupTown is not a bank, lender, insurer, investment adviser, or government service.
