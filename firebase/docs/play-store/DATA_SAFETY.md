# Google Play Data safety answers

Use these answers only after checking them against the final production build and vendor configuration. Google’s labels can change, so match the wording shown in the Play Console.

## Security and general declarations

- **Does the app collect or share user data?** Yes.
- **Is all collected user data encrypted in transit?** Yes, for the app/backend and provider connections using HTTPS/TLS. Confirm the final Hubtel and web endpoints are HTTPS.
- **Can users request deletion of data?** Only select Yes after the account-deletion process in `ACCOUNT_DELETION.md` is live. Until then, do not claim a completed deletion mechanism.
- **Is data used for advertising, advertising measurement, or marketing?** No.

## Data types not currently used by GroupTown

Do not select location, contacts, audio, health/fitness, calendar, browsing history, or advertising identifiers unless a future build adds those features. The app uses the device photo/document picker only after the member chooses media and grants permission.

## Sharing notes

Describe Firebase/Google Cloud, Hubtel, Expo notification delivery, and Sentry as service providers used to provide app functionality. Group/community content is shared with the audience selected by the member and with authorised administrators. It is not sold.

## Important review

The payment provider may process payment credentials on its hosted checkout page. GroupTown should be described as storing payment transaction metadata and status, not card or mobile-money credentials. Recheck this statement against the final Hubtel agreement.
