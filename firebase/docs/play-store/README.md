# GroupTown Google Play submission pack

This folder contains the text and answers needed for the GroupTown Google Play Console setup. It is based on the current mobile app and Firebase code as of 27 October 2026.

Before submitting, replace every `[REPLACE ...]` value, publish the privacy policy at a public HTTPS URL, create a dedicated reviewer account, and have the final legal text reviewed by the business/legal adviser.

Files:

- `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` — publish this as the public privacy-policy page.
- `ACCOUNT_DELETION.md` — required account-deletion process and Play Console declaration.
- `DATA_SAFETY.md` — field-by-field Data safety answers.
- `REVIEWER_ACCESS.md` — sign-in instructions for Google reviewers.
- `STORE_LISTING.md` — app name, descriptions, category, and listing copy.
- `CONTENT_RATING.md` — suggested questionnaire answers.
- `FINANCIAL_FEATURES.md` — financial-features declaration for Hubtel payments.
- `CLOSED_TEST_PLAN.md` — closed-test release and tester checklist.
- `SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md` — final pre-submission checklist.

Current product facts used in these documents:

- GroupTown is a housing/property group-ownership and programme-coordination app.
- Users can create an account with email/password or Google sign-in.
- The app collects profile, contact, date-of-birth, employment, next-of-kin, programme, and affordability information when users complete registration.
- Users can view homes and interests, join or create eligible groups, use community/group feeds and chat, upload photos, videos, and documents, receive notifications, submit support tickets, and view finance/payment history.
- Payments use a server-mediated Hubtel checkout. The app stores transaction status and reference information; Hubtel processes the chosen payment method.
- Firebase, Google sign-in, Expo notifications, Hubtel, and Sentry are service providers used by the app.
- There are no in-app advertisements, GPS/location features, contact-book access, health features, or government services.
- Registration is restricted to people aged 18 or older.
