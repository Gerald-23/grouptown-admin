# Financial features declaration

GroupTown includes payment functionality. In Play Console, do not declare the app as having no financial features.

Recommended selection, subject to the exact labels shown in your Console:

- **Mobile payments / digital payments:** Yes. Members can open a Hubtel hosted checkout and choose an available payment method.
- **Banking:** No. GroupTown does not provide bank accounts or banking services.
- **Lending or credit:** No. GroupTown does not issue loans or credit.
- **Cryptocurrency:** No.
- **Stock, investment, or financial advice:** No.
- **Insurance:** No.
- **Money transfer/remittance:** No, unless the final Hubtel configuration introduces a transfer product.

Explain that GroupTown stores payment transaction metadata and status for contributions, while Hubtel handles the hosted checkout and payment method credentials. Do not describe GroupTown as a bank, lender, wallet, or investment service.
